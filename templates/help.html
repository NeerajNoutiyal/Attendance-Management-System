AttendFlow Help & FAQ

How do I log in?

Use the login credentials provided for your account.

How do I add a student?

Log in as an administrator and use the student management section.

How do I mark attendance?

Select the student, choose the date and attendance status, then submit.

Can I export attendance records?

Use the attendance export option to download a CSV file.

What if I cannot log in?

Check your username and password. Contact the administrator if the problem continues.
