About AttendFlow

AttendFlow is a web-based Attendance Management System developed as an academic project.

Purpose

The system helps manage student records, record attendance, generate attendance reports and export records to CSV.

Key Features

  • Administrator and student login
  • Student record management
  • Attendance marking
  • Attendance reports
  • CSV export

Technology

Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML and CSS.
